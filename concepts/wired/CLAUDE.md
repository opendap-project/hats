# HAT Wired

Physical-interface R&D HAT: wired ports beyond USB as master, target and passive monitor, from fundamental buses to Gigabit Ethernet.

## Working here

- Requirements are edited in `docs/requirements/requirements.toml`; `docs/requirements/README.md` is rendered from it with `odapkit req render` and never edited by hand.
- This HAT mounts through the [OpenDAP HAT Standard](../../standard); follow it instead of redefining the interface here.
